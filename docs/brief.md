The community center currently works by people contacting the management team directly over a phone call. Then the clients have to fill out a form, which is physically done through pen-and-paper. Records often get lost or destroyed this way. Also, it leads to a waste of time and effort.

To solve this problem, a website is being built that will contain basic information about the community center along with pictures. It will have a form that a user can fill out to inquire about bookings on a specific date and time. The management team will receive this form and coordinate the final details over a call with the client.

This not only makes the booking process smooth and pen-and-paper free, but also helps with the publicity and scalability of the center.

We are deliberately not building a payment system, as the amount to be charged heavily depends on the duration, number of people, and usage of facilities like an A/V setup. The management hence wants all discussions to be over a phone call to finalize the price.
