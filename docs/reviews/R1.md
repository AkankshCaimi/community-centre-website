10. Guest count of 0, negative, text, or above 250 (FR5, FR2).
11. Duplicate and double submissions (FR5).

13. Session expiring mid-edit; no upper limit on an active session (NFR2).
14. Two admins editing one request; no record of who changed what (FR8).

### UK GDPR facts given by the reviewer (from memory of ICO guidance, not a live lookup; to be confirmed on ico.org.uk)

- No fixed retention period in law; the organisation must choose one, justify it and tell the client.
- Deletion and access requests must be acted on within one month, and are valid however they arrive, including by email.
- At the form the client must be told: who holds the data, why and on what legal basis, how long it is kept, their rights, and that they can complain to the ICO.
- A privacy notice is information, not an agreement. If consent is the legal basis, it must be a separate tick from the terms.
- A breach that risks harm to people must be reported to the ICO within 72 hours.

## Decisions (the author's)

### Booking and calendar

1. A request locks its whole calendar day from the moment it is submitted; one event per day.
2. An event that runs past midnight locks both days.
3. The lock is released when the request is declined.
4. A request with no decision after 48 hours is declined automatically and its day is freed. The 48 hours run through all days, including weekends.
5. Events can be requested at most 6 months in advance.
6. No past date or time can be selected. End time is always after start time. Minimum booking length is 1 hour.
7. The centre is assumed to be open 24 hours.

### States

8. A request can go straight from submitted to accepted or declined.
9. Backward moves are allowed: accepted to contacted, contacted to submitted. Accepted to declined is allowed.
10. Declined is final: a declined request can never change state.
11. Declining asks the admin for confirmation first.
12. Declined requests stay visible on the dashboard for reference.

### Notifications

13. The team is notified of a new request by email, and all details are on the admin page.
14. The client gets a confirmation email on submitting and on approval.
15. The client is not told when a request is declined or auto-declined.

### Abuse limits

16. CAPTCHA plus a submission limit.
17. At most 5 requests per day per network address. Over the limit, submit shows an error: too many responses submitted, try again later.
18. People sharing a network address share that allowance.
19. At most 2 slots at a time per person; a request counts if its phone number or its email matches an earlier one.

### Data protection

20. Retention is 6 months from the day of submission. Removal is automatic.
21. Having no client record on or soon after the event day is acceptable.
22. Admins can delete a client's data from the admin page. Clients ask for deletion by phone.
23. Deletion erases the personal details from the database; only the date and state remain.
24. The terms and the privacy notice are separate ticks on the form. The privacy notice says how to ask for deletion.
25. The team's notification emails are out of scope for retention.

### Admin and operations

26. One admin account for now, linked to an email, used by one person.
27. Password reset is by email.
28. Nothing happens after repeated wrong password attempts.
29. No backups for now.

### Form input

30. Hard bounds on guest count.
31. A maximum character length on every field, with validity and data-type checks.
